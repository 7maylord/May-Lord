## A Little About Me

I’m a software engineer who enjoys building products that solve difficult problems and survive outside the comfort of localhost.

My core stack is **TypeScript, Solidity and Rust**, and I work across the full development lifecycle—from system design and backend architecture to frontend implementation, testing, and deployment.

I build:

- Secure and scalable backend services with **NestJS, Node.js, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Redis**
- Fast, responsive web applications with **Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS**
- Asynchronous Rust services with **Axum, Tokio, SQLx, PostgreSQL, JWT, and Argon2**
- AI agents and autonomous workflows that analyse data, make decisions, and perform useful actions
- Payment, fintech, and blockchain applications with secure on-chain and off-chain integrations
- Smart contracts across Ethereum, Starknet, Arbitrum, Polkadot, and Stellar ecosystems

My blockchain work spans several languages and ecosystems:

- **Solidity** for Ethereum and EVM-compatible networks
- **Cairo** for Starknet
- **Rust** with Arbitrum Stylus and Polkadot ink!
- **Soroban** for Stellar smart contracts
- **Foundry, Hardhat, Viem, and The Graph** for testing, deployment, integrations, and blockchain data

I care about clean architecture, thoughtful abstractions, strong testing, readable code, and building systems that other engineers can maintain without needing archaeological equipment.

I’m currently interested in opportunities involving **full-stack engineering, backend systems, Rust, AI agents, fintech, developer tooling, and blockchain infrastructure**.
